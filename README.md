# Hi, I'm Jessica He

I work at the intersection of **data engineering, quantitative research, and applied machine learning**. My projects focus on building company data infrastructure, strengthening financial forecasting research, and developing AI agents for factor discovery and trading support.

I am studying **Computer Information Systems with a minor in Mathematics & Statistics at Cal Poly Pomona ('27)**, following earlier study in **Economics and Finance at Hong Kong Baptist University**.

## Current Focus

* **AI Agent for Factor Discovery**: Building an autonomous research workflow that explores financial data fields, generates economically motivated factor hypotheses, constructs candidate alphas, runs constrained backtests, and iteratively refines promising signals
* **Trading AI Agent**: Developing an auditable trading copilot that retrieves current information, identifies market and position states, refuses to act on incomplete inputs, calculates position size and downside risk under explicit rules, challenges each trade with counterarguments, and records every decision in a structured Trade Card
* **Financial Data Engineering**: Expanding the financial data warehouse into a company-wide data platform that unifies data across business functions, standardizes core metrics, and supports scalable reporting, analytics, and decision-making
* **Financial Research and RAG**: Strengthening cryptocurrency forecasting research through more rigorous, leakage-aware modeling and evaluation, with plans to incorporate RAG for retrieving historically similar market events and improving context-aware predictions
