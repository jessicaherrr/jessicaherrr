# Hi, I'm Jessica He
I work at the intersection of **quantitative research, data engineering, and applied machine learning**. My projects focus on building financial research systems, developing data infrastructure, and designing AI-assisted workflows for market analysis, factor discovery, and systematic trading research.

I am studying **Computer Information Systems with a minor in Mathematics & Statistics at Cal Poly Pomona ('27)**, following earlier study in **Economics and Finance at Hong Kong Baptist University**.

## Current Focus
* **News Alpha Portfolio Agent**: Building a multi-asset quantitative research system that transforms real-world news and events into economic mechanisms, measurable signals, portfolio hypotheses, deterministic backtests, and reusable scientific evidence.
* **Trading AI Agent**: Developing an auditable trading copilot that retrieves current information, identifies market and position states, refuses to act on incomplete inputs, calculates position size and downside risk under explicit rules, challenges each trade with counterarguments, and records decisions in structured Trade Cards.
* **Financial Data Engineering**: Expanding a financial data warehouse into a broader company data platform that unifies data across business functions, standardizes core metrics, and supports scalable reporting, analytics, and decision-making.
* **Financial Research and RAG**: Strengthening cryptocurrency forecasting research through more rigorous, leakage-aware modeling and evaluation, with plans to incorporate retrieval-augmented generation for historically similar market events and context-aware forecasting.
